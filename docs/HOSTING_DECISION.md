Railway documents the shared-IP caveat in [Static Outbound IPs](https://docs.railway.com/networking/static-outbound-ips) and lists current plan minimums on [Pricing](https://railway.com/pricing). Liquid Web's [managed VPS packages](https://www.liquidweb.com/vps-hosting/packages/) document management, dedicated IP and Acronis capacity; its [general pricing](https://www.liquidweb.com/pricing/) advertises cheaper VPS options whose managed total needs confirmation. Prices are observations on this date, not a quote or purchase authorization.

Decision gates before any migration: confirmed dedicated outbound IPv4, an acceptable recurring quote, Python service support, backup/restore proof, planned patch/reboot ownership, and a provider escalation path. Obtain those confirmations before purchasing. Postgres and automatic failover remain separate work.
